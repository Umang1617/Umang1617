## 🛠️ Things I've built

### 🦞 Clawdia
A multi-agent competitive intelligence system. Five agents watch six competitors across Reddit and gaming/AI communities, then send a weekly report and content calendar to Telegram.
**Built with:** OpenClaw, Node.js, Claude, Apify
🏆 2nd place at the OpenClaw Hackathon 2026

### 📊 GMIAS: Gaming Market Intelligence Automation System
A leaner, single-pipeline market intelligence tool. It runs as a Google Apps Script, uses the Gemini API, and delivers a daily market report through Google Docs and Gmail.
**Built with:** Google Apps Script, Gemini API

### 🧩 BlueAI Skills
Three skills for BlueAI, the assistant inside BlueStacks. Each one turns a single plain-language prompt into a finished answer.

- 🎮 **Pro Guide Finder:** type a character and a game, get the best build in one reply: three build options, why the top one works, how to play it, a tradeoffs table, and a note on how fresh the sources are.
- 🗺️ **Quest Guide** *(BlueAI Skillathon 2026)*: stuck on a boss or level? It opens Chrome inside BlueStacks, searches, reads the Google AI Overview, finds the best walkthrough, and returns the site, the link, and a 3-line strategy.
- ✈️ **Travel Planner** *(with Ayush)*: tell it where, from where, and when. You get cheap flights (Skyscanner India), three budget hotels (Booking.com), top attractions, and a budget comparison table.

## 🧠 The playbook: AI agents + data for community management

The loop I keep coming back to:

1. **Listen.** Social listening across Reddit, X and Facebook to catch sentiment shifts and user pain points early.
2. **Analyze.** Scraped trend data and competitor signals turned into weekly insights for product and marketing.
3. **Automate.** Agents and bots handle the repetitive work: reports, moderation, content calendars.
4. **Create.** Humans (me, mostly) spend the saved time on the creative stuff: events, storytelling, community vibes.

I write about this on [Medium](https://medium.com/@umang_writes) and share the visuals on [Behance](https://www.behance.net/umangsrivastava7).
